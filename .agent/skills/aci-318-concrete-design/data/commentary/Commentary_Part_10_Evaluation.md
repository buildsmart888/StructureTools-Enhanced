**CHAPTER 27—STRENGTH EVALUATION OF EXISTING STRUCTURES**

**27.1—Scope**

27.1.1 Provisions of this chapter shall apply to strength evaluation of existing structures by analytical means or by load testing.

**27.2—General**

27.2.1 If there is doubt that a part or all of a structure meets the safety requirements of this Code and the structure is to remain in service, the structure is to be evaluated and approved.

27.2.2 If the effect of a strength deficiency is well understood or if an analytical or empirical procedure is available to predict the structural behavior based on the existing analysis, an analytical evaluation of strength based on this information is permitted with the approval of the licensed design professional.

27.3.3 If the effects of a strength deficiency is not well understood or if it is not practical to compute the behavior of the structure and determine the material properties of the members required for analysis, a load test is required after approval by the licensed design professional.

27.4.4 If uncertainty about the strength or part or all of a structure involves deterioration, and if the observed response of a test does not confirm that the structural adequacy, such part of the structure is permitted to remain in service for a limited duration, if approved by the licensed design professional.

27.4.5 If the structure under investigation does not satisfy the requirements of a load test or analysis, and if approved by the licensed design professional, periodic reevaluations shall be conducted.
