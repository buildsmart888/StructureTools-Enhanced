## 6.6.4.5 Moment magnification method: Nonsway frames

**6.6.4.5.1** The factored moment used for design of columns and walls, $M_c$, shall be the value obtained from the moment magnification procedure of 6.6.4.5.2.

$$M_c = \delta M_2 \qquad (6.6.4.5.1)$$

**6.6.4.5.2** Magnification factor $\delta$ shall be calculated by:

$$\delta = \frac{C_m}{1 - \frac{P_u}{0.75P_c}} \ge 1.0 \qquad (6.6.4.5.2)$$

**6.6.4.5.3** $C_m$ shall be in accordance with (a) or (b).

(a) For columns with transverse loads between supports

$$C_m = 1 + 0.4 \frac{M_1}{M_2} \qquad (6.6.4.5.3a)$$

where $M_1/M_2$ is negative if the column is bent in single curvature and positive if the column is bent in double curvature, with the lesser absolute value, $M_1$, in the numerator. $C_m$ corresponds to the end moment with the lesser absolute value.

(b) For columns with transverse loads between supports:

$$C_m = 1.0 \qquad (6.6.4.5.3b)$$
