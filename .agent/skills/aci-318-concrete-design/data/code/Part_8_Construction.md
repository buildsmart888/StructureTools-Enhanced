### R26.1—Scope

This chapter establishes the minimum requirements, included in construction or contract documents, that are applicable to the project. The requirements include information developed in the structural design that must be communicated to the contractor. It also includes contractor or required quality and inspection requirements to verify compliance with the construction documents.

References in ACI 318 and references to specific USA consensus standards are presumed to be generally standard American practice. Other standards or compliance documents developed for the same purpose may be applicable to the Work or required by the building official or authority having jurisdiction. Therefore, the Code is written to be consistent with the requirements of the ICC.

---

(a) and (b) Except for the inspection requirements of Section 26.15, the contents of this chapter are intended to be design submissions and compliance requirements. These documents must be supplementary to and be properly coordinated with the construction drawings.
