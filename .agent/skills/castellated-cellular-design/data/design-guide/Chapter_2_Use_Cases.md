## 2.2 APPLICATIONS AND ADVANTAGES

The most common application of castellated and cellular beams is in span-long spans to long distances utilizing a lighter weight section. In pre-engineered metal buildings, these systems generally have 30 ft and greater bay sizes and are located at mid-bay area supporting rafters. Castellated and cellular beams can also span long distances and are typically top-loaded spanning over aisles in retail centers or industrial environments. Castellated and cellular beams can provide a more open floor space when there are changes. Advantages of span-to-depth ratios in beams include the ability to have the greater ceiling height without a deeper beam while in long spans, allowing for aesthetically pleasing geometry with minimal depth.

Lighter beams provide a cost savings with the unit weight over floor-space flexibility. The ability to run longer, lighter spans provides not only less material, but given system, service openings can be run through the beam openings. These beams are ideal for structures with long open spans requiring a minimum of parking spaces, industrial warehouse applications, and auditorium or place-of-assembly structures.
