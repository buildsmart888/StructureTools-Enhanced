## Notes

1. All references follow standard technical citation format.
2. References span from 1934 (earliest patents) to 2016 (current AISC standards).
3. Major contributing institutions include:
   - McGill University (Redwood, Aglan, Demirdjian)
   - University of Kansas (Darwin, Donahey)
   - Steel Construction Institute, UK (Knowles, Lawson, Ward)
   - AISC (design guides and standards)
   - Villanova University (Dinehart, Hoffman, Gross, Yost)

4. Key research periods:
   - 1934-1964: Initial development and patents
   - 1957-1980: Foundational experimental research
   - 1980-2000: Design method development
   - 2000-present: Refinement and codification

5. International contributions from:
   - Canada (McGill University, University of Toronto)
   - United Kingdom (Steel Construction Institute)
   - United States (universities and AISC)
   - Europe (Eurocode development)

6. For most recent design guidance, refer to:
   - AISC Design Guide 31 (2016) - this guide
   - AISC 360-16 Specification
   - AISC Steel Construction Manual, 15th Ed. (2016)
